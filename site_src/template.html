Evaluate your method

Paste the operating points of your method. The score, the position and every comparison are computed in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

1

Paste the rate–distortion points of your method

For each test set, paste the average rate and PSNR at each operating point. Fill one test set or all three; each is scored on its own.

2

Read the result

Scores are BD-rates against the VTM reference on the fixed window; negative means less rate. The position counts the entries on the board with a better score.

3

Add per-image records optional

With one record per image and operating point, a paired interval over the test images replaces the assumed variability. The comparison uses the eight releases measured from public checkpoints, so measure the same way: bits of the written bitstream over the original image area, PSNR of the 8-bit RGB reconstruction.

4

Submit to the board

An entry is ranked once its numbers are public: name the repository, the commit and the file that hold them. The check recomputes the score with the reference tool and looks for the numbers at that place.
